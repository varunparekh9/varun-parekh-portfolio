How I think

Good customer work is a system, not a series of heroic saves.

The best customer teams do more than react. They create repeatable ways to detect risk, drive adoption, and scale what works.

I like roles where I can move between the relationship and the operating model: listen to the customer, read the data, decide what matters, then build a process that makes the next outcome easier to achieve.

01

Find the signal early.

02

Make value visible.

Customers stay and expand when the product is tied clearly to a business outcome they can feel or measure.

03

Turn wins into a motion.

If a tactic works once, document it. If it works repeatedly, make it a playbook the whole team can use.
